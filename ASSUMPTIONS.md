# BETCCO implementation assumptions

- BETCCO remains an independent education platform. References to BTEC describe the learning pathway only and do not imply a Pearson affiliation.
- Public teaching counts, student counts, course counts, course prices, progress, cart contents, evaluation assignments, and notifications are read from the API and database. The interface does not manufacture ratings, instructor profiles, enrollment totals, or testimonials when the platform has no such data.
- Motion uses the installed GSAP tooling. It is strongest on public learning and marketing surfaces; dashboard and player movement is restrained and can be disabled through the learner's display preference or the operating-system reduced-motion preference.
- The deep-space canvas is decorative only, has no audio, caps device pixel ratio at 2, pauses while the tab is hidden, and renders a static alternative when motion is reduced.
- Brand text is read from public `SiteSettings` with `frontend/src/lib/brand.ts` as the safe fallback. The default product identity is BETCCO.
- The external payment provider remains behind the existing payment abstraction. The local `Fake` provider is available only for development/test webhook verification; enrollment is not created from a client success URL.
- Each confirmed paid course sale is allocated on the server after the verified webhook: 30% of the net amount is credited to the platform wallet and 70% to the course teacher. Coupon discount is allocated proportionally across paid courses before that split.
- Teacher withdrawal destinations are encrypted with ASP.NET Core Data Protection and are masked in the administrator wallet. A withdrawal reserves the teacher balance immediately; an administrator may approve, reject (which restores the balance), or execute it.
- Card, bank-transfer, and e-wallet checkout choices are development/test adapters until verified external providers are implemented. The local payout adapter never transfers money; production safely refuses a payout until a provider adapter and its credentials are configured.
- `BETCCO_NEXT_DIST_DIR` is an optional local/CI build-output override. It exists because the OneDrive workspace can retain a stale Next metadata-route output; normal builds continue to use `.next` when the variable is absent.
- The legal package is seeded as versioned, API-backed documents (version 1.0, effective 25 August 2026) and is operational content rather than legal advice. The owner is centrally set to HAMZA QAHIR ALSHWATER; registration number, postal address, payment provider, and legal contact inboxes intentionally retain conspicuous pre-publication placeholders until the owner supplies verified values.
- Self-registration stores country, phone, gender, and date of birth and is restricted server-side to learners aged 18 or over. A verified parent/guardian-consent workflow is still required before BETCCO can accept registrations from minors in production.
- Development/Test uses a deliberate clean-file scanner so local course and evaluation flows remain testable. Production rejects uploads unless a ClamAV daemon is explicitly configured; a rejected or unavailable scan never creates a private submission file.
- A BTEC task rubric is section-aware: its persisted criterion codes are `A.P1`–`A.P4`, `A.M1`–`A.M3`, and `A.D1`–`A.D3`, repeated for Sections B and C. The teacher selects the applicable criteria per section; each selected section needs Pass criteria, and selecting Distinction also requires Merit in that same section.
- Evaluation grades are calculated on the server, never accepted from the browser. A completed Pass/Merit/Distinction section reports 60%/80%/100%; the task's final award is the lowest completed section award and becomes student-visible only after the administrator's internal verification.
- Course assignments will be modeled separately from the existing paid external `EvaluationRequest`: a course assignment belongs to a teacher's course and supports coursework submissions, while an evaluation request is a paid, administrator-assigned BTEC assessment service. This preserves each workflow's ownership, payment, and audit boundaries instead of reusing one entity for two incompatible processes.
- `CourseModule` remains the persisted compatibility name for the platform's earlier module model, while the BETCCO interface presents it as a BTEC Unit. A unit may now contain learning aims, topics, BTEC criteria, and lessons without requiring a destructive migration of existing course content.
- Course quizzes can be attached to a specific Quiz lesson or remain course-level. Their correct answers are available only to an authorized teacher authoring the quiz; student attempts receive only the scoring result that the server permits.
- Fill-in-the-blank, ordering, and matching questions are auto-graded entirely on the server. Matching pairs use the authoring format `left => right`; learners receive only the left prompts and an unordered pool of right-side choices, never the stored pair mapping.
- Quiz delivery settings are enforced by the API: availability dates, late-attempt policy, attempt limit, and time limit cannot be bypassed by changing browser time or requests. Question and option randomization is deterministic per learner, keeping the same order when they resume an attempt while avoiding a shared order across learners.
- A course assignment may narrow the safe upload types and per-file limit, but it can never exceed 100MB per file or enable an executable type. Existing coursework was safely backfilled to the platform defaults (PDF, Office documents, images, ZIP, and TXT; 100MB; resubmission enabled) when this policy was introduced.
- Quiz settings remain editable only while a quiz is a draft and has no attempts. Once learners have attempted or a quiz is published, its delivery and scoring configuration is preserved as part of the academic record.
- Administrator analytics uses the selected date window for paid orders, paid revenue, refunds, and lesson-learning activity. Counts such as users, current courses, current assignments, pending reviews, and enrollment completion remain live platform-state metrics so that an older time filter cannot hide work that still needs an administrator's action.
- Essay and code quiz answers are deliberately never executed or auto-graded. They remain in a teacher-owned manual-review queue; the server releases a final quiz score only after one 0–100 mark has been recorded for every manual question.
- An image quiz question can reference only a clean image resource already uploaded to the same course. The student receives it through an authenticated quiz endpoint after enrollment checks, rather than through a public storage URL.
- Two-factor authentication uses the ASP.NET Core Identity authenticator-token provider (standard time-based one-time passwords). BETCCO deliberately shows a one-time manual setup key instead of persisting a QR image or secret; the key is stored only by Identity's protected token store, never in audit logs or custom session records.
- Each new login receives an opaque, signed cookie claim linked to a server-side `UserSession` record. Session activity is refreshed at most once every five minutes; users may end one session or all sessions, and the latter also rotates the Identity security stamp so legacy cookies without a session record are invalidated.
- Course-assignment and certificate email events are sent through a replaceable, best-effort adapter only after the server has persisted the academic state. An unavailable SMTP provider cannot roll back a submission, grade, resubmission request, or certificate; delivery is not represented as a success claim without a configured provider.
- Content drip and prerequisite decisions are evaluated by the API for the course player, lesson files, lesson progress, quizzes, and coursework submissions. The browser receives only a localized lock reason and never grants access by itself.
- The learner calendar combines private planner entries with server-owned assignment due dates, quiz windows, and published live sessions. Only a learner's own private calendar entries can be deleted; academic events link back to the relevant enrolled course.
- Course announcements are private to enrolled learners. A teacher can target every enrolled learner, a BTEC Unit context, or a validated selected-enrollee subset; the API persists the announcement and notifications before attempting best-effort email delivery.
- A question-bank item belongs to one teacher and one course. Adding it to a draft quiz copies an immutable question snapshot, so later bank edits or deletions cannot change a published quiz or past learner attempt.
- Certificate QR images are generated locally by the API from the public verification URL. `APP_PUBLIC_URL` must be set for deployed environments; development falls back to the current request origin and does not call a third-party QR service.
- Membership and course-subscription purchases create time-bounded server-side enrollments. A one-time course or package purchase clears that expiry and becomes permanent; all learner-facing access checks reject expired entitlements.
- Membership-plan and course-subscription configuration is administered through authenticated administrator APIs. Plans contain only published courses, payment stores a snapshot of the included course allocation and billing interval, and the same verified webhook path performs fulfillment.
- Coupon redemption is counted only after payment confirmation. The server applies dates, course applicability, minimum purchase, global maximum, and per-learner maximum; it rechecks usage while handling the webhook so a stale checkout cannot exceed a limit.
- Live-class URLs and recording URLs must use HTTPS. A student attendance row is created only after the API confirms that the learner is entitled to join the session; administrators can review attendance without exposing learner details publicly.
- Memberships, course subscriptions, and coupons are now also manageable through the administrator interface. Their price, availability, entitlement, and coupon-use limits still remain server-owned; the interface never sends a final price or marks a payment successful.
- A teacher can add an HTTPS learning-resource link alongside a privately uploaded file. Links are surfaced only after the same enrollment and drip/prerequisite checks as files; uploaded files remain private and are not moved to a public web root.
- A lesson marked as a published preview is intentionally accessible to visitors through a dedicated, server-gated preview endpoint. Its clean downloadable resources are exposed only through that explicit preview decision; all non-preview course content remains enrollment-gated.
- Joining a live class automatically records Present or Late after a ten-minute grace period. An administrator may correct an existing or course-enrolled learner's status to Present, Late, Absent, or Excused, and that change is audit logged.
- Question-bank matching entries use the same normalized `left => right` validation as regular quizzes. Reused questions therefore cannot create ambiguous matching pairs in a generated examination.
- Package availability windows, package prices, and package savings are server-owned; unpublished, expired, or not-yet-open packages cannot be added to a cart or checked out.
- Audit-log browsing is read-only, administrator-only, paginated, and does not expose sensitive request bodies.
- Audit events are enriched by the persistence layer with the request correlation ID, source IP, and a length-bounded user agent. Old/new values are intentionally limited to non-sensitive operational state (for example account approval or freeze state), never credentials, tokens, upload keys, or private file content.
- The administrator controls the private `PlatformCommissionPercent` setting (default 30%). It is read only by the verified server-side payment workflow, applies only to future confirmed sales, and snapshots each allocation so a later setting change cannot rewrite settled accounting.
- Course bundles are managed as the existing `CoursePackage` aggregate. Administrators can edit a saved bundle's descriptive content, price, published state, availability window, and published-course membership; the server validates the complete replacement before changing any links and records a safe audit snapshot.
- Live classes use a replaceable provider catalog. The current adapters validate administrator-supplied HTTPS URLs for Manual, Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Jitsi; they do not create meetings or contact a vendor. A future managed-meeting adapter must be registered behind `ILiveSessionProvider` and keep its API credentials in environment configuration, never in source control.
- `LessonType.Activity` was appended to the existing enum so persisted lesson-type values retain their meaning. Activities use the normal localized lesson body, private resources, access rules, completion tracking, and course-player protections; a future interactive activity engine can add activity-specific configuration without weakening those controls.
- Quiz and coursework authoring now retain an explicit `Draft`, `Published`, `Scheduled`, or `Archived` lifecycle while preserving the legacy `IsPublished` flag for safe compatibility. Existing published rows are migrated to `Published`; scheduled content is released only by the server-side publisher when its configured opening time arrives.
- Coursework briefs may include teacher-uploaded resources stored under private storage. An enrolled learner can download a clean resource only after the assignment is published and passes the same server-side access rule used for the assignment; teachers and administrators retain their authorized access.
- A coursework grade may contain student-visible feedback per BTEC criterion, student-visible overall feedback, and separate private notes. Private notes are deliberately filtered from every learner response and are shown only to the reviewing teacher and platform administration.
- Learning-aim progress and predicted BTEC grade are calculated from persisted lesson completion and criterion results on the server. The administrative gradebook is read-only, paginated, and filterable by course, unit, teacher, student, submission status, grade, date range, and search text.
- Verified course, membership, and course-subscription payments now create a server-owned enrollment notification and invoke the replaceable enrollment-email adapter only after the payment transaction commits. Duplicate webhook events do not repeat either outcome.
- Coursework deadline reminders are dispatched by a server-clock background worker every fifteen minutes when a published assignment enters its final 24 hours. A per-user database deduplication key prevents duplicate in-app or email reminders across retries or worker restarts; an email failure remains non-critical and never changes academic state.
- A teacher-uploaded lesson video is limited to MP4 or WEBM and 500MB in the local/private-storage implementation. It is malware-scanned before storage, is selected server-side as the lesson's stream source, and is delivered only from teacher- or enrollment-authorized video endpoints with range support. For production-scale or resumable multi-gigabyte uploads, a reviewed private object-storage upload adapter must replace this direct multipart path; no public video URL is assumed.

## 2026-08-28 — Sales tax

- BETCCO does not assume a tax or VAT percentage from a jurisdiction. `SalesTaxPercent` is an administrator-controlled, server-side setting seeded at `0` until an authorized legal/accounting decision is made.
- The server snapshots tax on each `Payment`; teacher and platform revenue allocations exclude that tax.

## 2026-08-28 — Public catalog cache

- Only published public-course lists and published course-detail DTOs are cached in-process for 30 seconds. Search-term requests, private learning data, progress, payments, uploads, permissions, and grades bypass this cache.
